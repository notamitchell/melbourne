Born here, died there

Where the people in eMelbourne were born and where they died, for the places in Melbourne and Victoria that can be pinned down with certainty. Places overseas and interstate are left off the map. A line joins a birthplace to a place of death. Pick a span of years or a line of work, and click a dot to see who it was.
