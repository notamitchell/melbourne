Experiments

  • Lifespans

    Every person as a bar from birth to death, coloured by kind of work, with how many were alive each year. From person/birth, death and occupation.

  • Related records

    The records one to three links from any record: related entries, themes, and the places people were born or died in. From listRelation and placeName/@ref.

  • Themes together

    Which of the 21 themes are tied by related records more often than chance. From relation[@type='theme'] and the related entries.

  • Where people were born

    The commonest birthplaces and places of death, and which have their own place page. From birth/placeName and death/placeName.

  • On this day

    Births, deaths and digital objects for every day of the year, as a calendar; opens on today. From birth/@when, death/@when and the object dates in data/objects.js.

  • Who wrote what

    The authors of eMelbourne entries against the themes they wrote on. From titleStmt/author and the themes.

  • Objects through time

    The 1,561 images and digitised records by year, with the entries they belong to. From data/objects.js.
