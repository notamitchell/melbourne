Lifespans

Every person in eMelbourne as a bar from birth to death, coloured by the kind of work they did. The line above counts how many of the people shown were alive in each year. Point at a bar for details; click it to open the entry.

Kinds of work are worked out from each person's occupations in the TEI (608 different occupations, folded into eight groups by keyword); a person goes in the group of their first occupation that fits. Dates known only to the year are drawn from the start of that year.
