Experiments

Lines of work

What the people in eMelbourne did for a living. Many had more than one line of work, so the first chart shows which ones went together, and the second shows which were common among people born in each decade. Click any work to see the people.

Work that went together

Each circle is a line of work held by at least four people; bigger circles are held by more. A line joins two kinds of work when at least two people did both, and is thicker when more did. Colours show the broad field.

Rise and fall

The most common lines of work, by the decade people were born in. The bolder the square, the bigger the share of everyone born in that decade. Point at a square for the numbers, or click it to see the people.

Women and men

The most common lines of work for the women and for the men in eMelbourne, as a share of each. Far fewer women have entries, so their bars rest on smaller numbers.
