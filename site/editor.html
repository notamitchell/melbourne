          How publishing works

          1. Open or start an entry here and make your changes. Your work is kept in this browser as you go, so a closed tab isn't lost.
          2. Use Preview to see the page as it will look, and Compare to see what you changed.
          3. Press Save files. Two files go to your Downloads folder: the entry (.xml) and a copy the site loads (.js).
          4. Upload both files to the eMelbourne repository on GitHub, through the GitHub website. Nothing changes on the site until you do.

          The full guide is EDITING.md.
